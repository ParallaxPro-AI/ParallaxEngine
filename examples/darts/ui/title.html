TungstenAlley

How to Play

Walk the reticle onto the treble you want, then hold the meter and let go on a settled hand. Release inside the green band and the dart flies true; outside it, the dart scatters. Gravity drops it and the draught pushes it, so aim a touch high and into the wind. Close every leg on exactly zero inside the dart budget. Go below zero or leave 1 and it's a bust.
